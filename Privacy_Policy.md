Constellation the app is a mobile game developed by Arlen. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

We do not directly collect or store personal information. However, third-party services used in the app may collect data as described below.

Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Unity IAP (In-App Purchases)
We use Unity's in-app purchasing system to process purchases. Purchase transactions are handled by Google Play and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Games Services
If you choose to sign in, Google Play Games may collect your Google account profile information. This is optional. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage
Game progress is stored locally on your device using encrypted preferences. We do not have access to this data.

Children's Privacy
This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.
