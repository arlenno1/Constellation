How to Play

Four rules. Infinite puzzles.

★
One star per row. Every row of the grid holds exactly one.
★
One star per column. Same rule, turned sideways.
★
One star per region. Each coloured zone gets exactly one, wherever it lands.
★
No touching. Not edge to edge, not corner to corner. Stars keep their distance.
